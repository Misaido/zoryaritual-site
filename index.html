What it does

Less a beauty planner. More a piece of infrastructure for looking after yourself.

A real library, and a calendar that understands it

Hundreds of treatments across every category, from injectables and lasers to lash lifts, at-home devices, and hair care. Book something once, set it recurring, and see it all in week, month, or year view. Pick a time that conflicts with something already scheduled, and Zorya tells you while you're still choosing it, not after you've committed.

Daily care, sequenced properly

Build your morning and evening routines, check off each step, and get a countdown when something needs to sit before the next layer goes on. New actives go on a ladder that steps you up gently, and back down if your skin objects.

A safety net that stays quiet until it matters

When two things are scheduled too close together, you get a note, not a wall. A pregnancy-safe mode swaps out what it needs to. Recovery Mode notices when you've had a peel or laser, and eases your stronger actives back in as you heal.

Event prep, worked backwards from the date

Tell Zorya about a wedding, a shoot, or a holiday, and it works backwards from the date: what to start, what to taper, what to stop in time.

Reminders that respect you

Stack the frequencies you want, set quiet hours, and Zorya bundles extras into one nudge rather than pecking at you all day.

How Zorya Recommends works

Zorya does not take sides between brands. It does not care whose serum you own, and it will never tell you the thing on your shelf is wrong so you go buy a replacement.

But a routine has gaps. New users especially. It is hard to know which brands actually contain a real, verified dose of an active, and which are mostly marketing. You fill in your steps and there is one you have not sorted yet. Or your desired results would best be supported by a quality at home LED mask, or your hair keeps snagging on a cotton pillowcase night after night. That is when a suggestion shows up. A product, or a tool, sometimes a whole treatment category, sitting next to the part of your routine it belongs to. It won't look like an ad, and it definitely won't feel like a shop got bolted onto the side of your skincare app.

Fit comes first. If Zorya earns a commission on something it points you to, it says so, right there on the pick, every single time. It won't be buried inside a tip or dressed up as neutral advice. And if you would rather not see any of it, you switch the picks off and nothing else about the app changes.

That is the whole deal. Helpful when you need it, honest when there is money involved, and out of your way otherwise.

Some recommendations contain affiliate links. Where they do, you will always be told.

Privacy

Yours, and private.

Medical history stays on your device

There is no table for it in our database. What you record about conditions, medications, or pregnancy shapes your schedule locally. We cannot read it, cannot lose it in a breach, and could not hand it over if asked.

Works fully offline

Your routines and schedule sync later, whenever you're back online. Nothing about using Zorya requires a live connection.

Export or delete, any time

Take everything you've entered with you, or remove it entirely, without emailing anyone to ask permission.

Calendar access, minimized

When Zorya reads your calendar to schedule around your life, it takes only which days are busy. Never what the events are.
