Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

This page covers this website. It is short, because the site does very little.

What this site collects

Nothing, unless you type your email into the waitlist form and submit it. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no tracking of how you move around the page.

If you join the waitlist

We store the email address you give us, and nothing else. We use it for one purpose: to tell you when the beta opens up. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

You can be removed at any time by emailing hello@zoryaritual.com. We will delete the address rather than mark it inactive, and we do not need a reason.

Who else touches your visit

A static website still passes through other people's servers. In the interest of being straight with you, here is the full list:

  • GitHub Pages hosts these files. GitHub receives standard web server request data, including your IP address.
  • Cloudflare handles the domain's DNS, and routes your request to the host.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces this site uses. Loading them sends your IP address to Google. If you would rather that did not happen, a font blocker or a privacy-focused browser will stop it, and the site still works.
  • Formspree receives your email address if, and only if, you submit the waitlist form.

The app is a separate matter

Zorya the application handles far more than an email address, and it is governed by its own privacy policy rather than this one. That policy is published with the app. The short version, which the app is actually built around: your medical history, questionnaire answers, and photos stay on your device and never reach us.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, the EU, or another region with data protection law, you have the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to how it is used. Given that what we hold is an email address you typed in yourself, exercising any of these is a one-line email to hello@zoryaritual.com.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
